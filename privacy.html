Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

Chamundi TV Live ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Permissions & Data Access

To provide live streaming functionality, our App requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used to capture video for live streaming. We do not record or store camera data properly on our servers; it is transmitted directly to your chosen streaming platform (e.g., YouTube, Facebook).
  • Microphone: Used to capture audio for live streaming.
  • Storage (Photos/Media/Files): Required to save and load stream configurations, overlays, and temporary cache files.
  • Network/Internet: Required to transmit the live stream to the destinations you configure.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We collect minimal data necessary for the App's operation:

  • Account Information: If you log in, we store your email address and authentication credentials securely using Supabase Authentication.
  • Stream Keys: Stream keys you enter or are assigned are stored securely on your device using industry-standard encryption. They are synchronized with our backend solely for the purpose of easier device setup.
  • Usage Logs: We may collect anonymous crash reports and performance data to improve App stability.

3. Third-Party Services

Our App enables you to stream content to third-party platforms such as YouTube and Facebook. Please be aware that when you stream to these services, their respective privacy policies and terms of service apply. We are not responsible for how these third parties handle your streamed content.

4. Data Security

We implement security measures designed to protect your information, including encrypting sensitive credentials (like stream keys) on your device. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes/updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@chamunditv.com
