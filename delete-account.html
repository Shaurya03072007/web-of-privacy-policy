How to delete your account

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Aurora LIVE Streaming app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings from the main menu.
  3. Tap on Account.
  4. Scroll down and select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice when prompted.

Option 2: Request Deletion

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion here. We will process your request within 30 days.
